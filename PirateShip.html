The Black Diamond

RPG Maker map - Captain Jacob Ravens' ship

This is Jacob's home as a vessel: occupation pirate, house the fastest ship of the seven seas. It sits next to ONE million, the campaign where this same captain sails beyond the Ice Wall.

Design Rationale

Every room is a job. Kitchen and drying rack are food. Fishing gear is a sustainable source of food. Cannons and weapons are how he stays a pirate. The escape raft is the honest fail state. Navigation table and captain's office are planning. Bedroom and the "toilet" are the private life the ship still has to contain. Bookshelves exist because a character who only fights is boring to live with.

The deck is a compact loop: entrance, cannons, kitchen/dining, storage, office/treasure, bedroom. Circulation is short on purpose. A ship map should feel fast, personal, and slightly ridiculous - wine next to the raft, crabs on the rail - without losing the functions that make Jacob able to leave port.
