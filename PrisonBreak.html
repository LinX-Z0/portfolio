Prison Break

Mega Man Maker — normal puzzle-style level

Prison Break is a normal-difficulty puzzle map. Unlike 4KOMA, which focuses two mobility skills, this one is allowed to be "busy": rooms ask you to juggle several tools at once. Primary theme is puzzle rooms. Secondary is juggling — roll, card throw, keys, breaks, water, midair dash, ice gun variants.

Design Rationale

Normal difficulty here means more mechanics, not meaner spikes. Checkpoints and safe spots sit under the parkour so a missed dash is a retry, not a run killer. The map teaches in layers: roll into the next room, then card throw for a key, then vertical throws, then throw-while-jumping, then midair dash as its own idea before combining dash with jump, water, and one-block gaps.

The ice gun is a late-map language shift. Freeze fire and walk on it (G), diagonal shot to wake a platform (G'), spread shot to hit multiple targets (G''). Same weapon, three precision grades, introduced after the player already has dash and throw in muscle memory. That is difficulty through mastery of a small kit, even though the kit is larger than 4KOMA's.

The last room is the hardest on purpose (intensity 7, midair dashes). Purple keys along the way look useless. If you bothered to pick them up, you skip that room. That is guided freedom: the "correct" path is still the intended gauntlet, but attention is a real alternate win condition, not a collectathon the game yells about.

Level systems / cadence

Sixteen labelled challenges across eight pages. Notation:

  • A roll
  • B card throw / B' vertical throw
  • C locked door that needs a key
  • D break block
  • E jump / E' jump in water
  • F midair dash / F' precise midair dash through a one-block gap
  • G ice gun / G' diagonal ice / G'' ice spread

Cadence is written as combinations, because the point of the map is combinations: B+C+D for the first real puzzle, E+F for dash-while-jumping, G then G' then G'' as a three-step weapon tutorial, F' as the precision dash exam before the ice sequence. Intensity climbs from 1 (just roll) to 6–7 in the dash and ice rooms, with a few 4s in between so it is not a straight staircase.

  • Secondary: juggling multiple skills
