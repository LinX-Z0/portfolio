Mega Man Maker

Three stages, one method. Cadence-named challenges, intensity scored per zone — then three different briefs.

Role

Timeline

Category
Level Design
  • Shared method — name the challenge in a cadence alphabet before building; score intensity per zone.
  • JumpMan — mobility + timing; two verbs getting stricter.
  • FIVENITE — easy combat; loadout freedom, break and build.
  • Prison Break — normal puzzle; juggle tools; purple-key skip on the hardest screen.

These are not three unrelated Maker uploads. They are one level-design practice run three ways: lock a brief, name every room in that language, then build only what the name asks for. If a room cannot be named, it does not ship.

Each stage has its own case study with the map document, cadence, and intensity tables.
