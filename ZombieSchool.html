Sunrise Call High

RPG Maker map - school turned zombie apocalypse base

When the outbreak hit, students and faculty of Sunrise Call High stayed put and split the campus by skill. The plan is a school that still reads as a school, with new functions bolted onto old rooms instead of a generic bunker skin.

Design Rationale

This is the programme I kept from the rejected military-base draft in my design intent: a school already has gyms, toilets, canteens, classrooms, and a drop-off. Reuse them. Classrooms become gender-split living quarters with radios. The drop-off becomes virus screening and quarantine. Gyms and workshops become training. A greenhouse and water filtration sit where a campus can actually grow food. Four executives (two faculty, two students) hold the hall-general meeting room. Specialists handle weapons in isolated side rooms.

The map is about factioned circulation and survival infrastructure, not a hallway of identical beds. You should be able to point at a room and know whether it is rest, food, defense, or command. That is theme-led layout with a clear function per wing.
