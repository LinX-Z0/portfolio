Sunrise Call High

RPG Maker. A school that still reads as a school, reused as a zombie-outbreak base.

Students and faculty stay on campus and split it by skill. I did not skin a bunker over the plan. Gyms, toilets, canteen, classrooms, and drop-off keep their bones and take new jobs.

Classrooms become living quarters. The drop-off becomes screening and quarantine. Gyms and workshops become training. Greenhouse and water filtration sit where a campus can actually grow food. Command sits in the hall. Weapons sit in isolated side rooms. You should be able to point at a wing and know rest, food, defense, or command.
