ONE million

Campaign design document. Five phases. Gold tables and a help-flag ending. Not a shipped RPG Maker game.

Captain Jacob Ravens has to bring Fountain of Youth water home before his mother dies. I designed the campaign as tables: events, intensity, and gold. Phases start with a hook spike or a recovery beat, then climb. Side content is optional intensity, not padding. His ship as a house map is The Black Diamond.

Five phases

  • Beyond the Ice Wall — teach ship and cannons; Kraken spike; wreck into prison.
  • The Great Escape — stealth, button maze, grappling, volcano exit.
  • Back on Track — hub. Two loud goals: earn a ship, learn the fountain. Quiet side quests. Off-route ocean bosses as warning and reward.
  • Heart of the Sea — buy the ship, gate and pyramid layers, boss.
  • Atlantis and Homecoming — fountain choice, optional true ending, return.

Gold and ending

Gold buys the next verb (a ship at 25,000 base). Totals step into the title: 80k accumulated, then 250k, 500k, 1,000,000, 1,325,000. Side quests pay enough to tempt a detour without stranding the golden path.

Take the water and the new world dies. Leave it and the mother dies. Help the people who asked along the way, and the fountain lets you take it. Side quests are that flag. The event table has to track who was helped.
