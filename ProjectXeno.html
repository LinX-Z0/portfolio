Project Xeno

CSD2183, Team 19. Predator AI study and prototype — Isolation's hunt without live machine learning.

Class research plus a playable prototype, not a full game. The question: Isolation's xenomorph feels like it learns you. What is the actual system, and how do you rebuild that feeling without an unfair learner the player cannot read?

Research and the presentation were team work. I owned the metric-gating spec, most of the NPC profiles, most of the code, and the bulk of the prototype. The four-layer architecture was a team decision; I was the one who tried to implement it. Blueprints were team.

What Isolation actually does

The xenomorph is not training a model mid-playthrough. Creative Assembly splits the brain. A Director AI is omniscient: it sets pacing and a menace gauge, and posts jobs onto a blackboard. A behavior tree is not omniscient: it only has senses, polls those jobs, and runs search, chase, and attack on its own. The player feels figured out. What they are playing against is a director managing fear plus a creature that is allowed to miss.

Spec

Avoid an unstoppable learner that outpaces the player's ability to adapt. Escalation should force new tactics, not an invisible cheat. The team framed an agent that tracks player metrics and unlocks gated behaviors over time — a systemic illusion of learning, not a neural net.

I specified the metric gates. Example meters: flamethrower uses, locker and vent hides, noise, time in line of sight, repeated escape routes. Pipeline: player action → metric ticks → threshold → gate opens → new behavior is legal. You can tune when the alien starts checking lockers without it inventing an unreadable cheat.

Four layers, each managing the one below: omniscient pacing, high-level strategy from senses, mid-level execution, low-level search / chase / attack. That split was the team's. I attempted it in the prototype.

NPCs are part of the experiment. I designed most of the three reaction profiles: Civilian (long delay, freezes), Technician (a beat to assess, then acts), Ghost (zero delay, tracks the xenomorph, stays out of the danger zone).

Prototype

I built most of the prototype and wrote most of the code. Blueprints were shared. The playable is a test bed for gating and NPC reactions, not a finished Isolation clone.
