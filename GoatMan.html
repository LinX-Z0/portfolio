Gary the Goat Man

RPG Maker house map. Goat workflow is the spine. Human rooms hang off it.

Gary is a goat farmer who built a goat-shaped house on a goat farm. The joke is the programme: the animals move first. People fit around that path, not the other way around.

Circulation

Goat highway: free roam and baby barn, milking and shearing, then butchery. Wool, milk, and meat storefronts turn that path into an economy. The kitchen wall opens into the baby barn so he can watch kids while he cooks. Living room, bedroom, and car are pockets off the loop. Crops, fertilizer, and compost outside close the feed cycle.

Role

  • Theme, programme, circulation, events

Stack

  • RPG Maker — purchased tileset
