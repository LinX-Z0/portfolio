Gary the Goat Man

RPG Maker map - a house shaped around goats

Gary is a part-time goat farmer and a full-time goat lover. When he could afford land, he built a goat-shaped house on a goat farm. The layout is the joke taken seriously: human life is fitted around animal workflow, not the other way around.

Design Rationale

The "goat highway" is the spine. Goats move from free roam and baby barn into milking and shearing, then down to the butchery when their time is up. Wool processing, washing, spinning, milk storefront, and meat storefront turn that path into an economy. Gary's kitchen wall is broken open into the baby goat barn so he can watch kids while he cooks. That is guided circulation: one readable loop, with living room, bedroom, and car as the human pockets hanging off it.

Outside, crop farm, fertilizer, and compost close the loop so the map can feed both Gary and the herd. The design brief from my intent write-up was exactly this: structure a house entirely around the needs of goats, and still make it playable as a space.
