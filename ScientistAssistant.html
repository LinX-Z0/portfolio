Tony and Bruce

RPG Maker. Two jobs, two wings.

Tony extracts DNA and rebuilds dinosaurs. He cannot cook, clean, or run a household. Bruce keeps both of them alive. The house keeps those jobs apart. If you only look at rooms, you still know who does what.

Circulation

Tony's wing: lab, incubator, analytics, hatchery, nicer bath. Bruce's wing: kitchen (Tony is banned), garage, fossil path, feeding, cleaning. Fossils enter through Bruce. DNA happens in Tony's rooms. Dinosaurs live where Bruce can feed them without walking through the lab.

Role

  • Theme, programme, circulation, events
