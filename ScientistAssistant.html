Tony and Bruce

RPG Maker map - scientist house as a two-character machine

Theme-led layout: Tony is a paleontologist who can extract DNA and reconstruct dinosaurs, and cannot cook, clean, or run a household. Bruce is the assistant who keeps both of them alive. The house is built so those two jobs never mix.

Design Rationale

Private space is split by role. Tony gets the scientist's room, a nicer bathroom, the DNA lab, incubator, analytics screen, and the dinosaur hatchery / observatory. Bruce gets the assistant's room, a smaller toilet, cleaning supplies, kitchen (Tony is banned), garage for fossil deliveries, feeding area, and the path from truck to fossil reserves. Circulation is the design: fossils come in through Bruce, DNA happens in Tony's wing, dinosaurs live where Bruce can feed them without walking through Tony's genius.

The map is a character document. If you only looked at rooms, you would still know who does what. That is the same "no idea is too crazy" brief as the rest of this RPG Maker set: make a mad-scientist workplace that still functions as a house.
