About Me

I'm a Game Design & Computer Science student at DigiPen Singapore. On team projects I've led design on tactics and card systems, built engine tooling in C++, and shipped gameplay from combat loops to fishing survival.

Outside of class I finish games thoroughly, then write down what I enjoyed most and least, and why. That habit feeds how I design. I care about what the player actually feels, not only what the spreadsheet says.
