About Me

I'm Lin Xin, a BSc undergraduate in IMGD at DigiPen Singapore, looking for a game design / level design internship.

I focus on clear, intentional player experiences: structuring each space, defining how mechanics are used, and thinking through what players should do, learn, and feel in every section.

My process usually starts from either theme-led design, where identity and story shape the space, or mechanics-led design, where abilities and interactions come first. I then iterate with programmes, skill themes, pacing curves, and playtest feedback.

I care about difficulty over complexity, guided freedom, and ideas that feel too unusual until they work.
