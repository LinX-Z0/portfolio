4KOMA

Mega Man Maker — mobility-focused level

4KOMA is a Mega Man Maker stage about getting good at two tools, not collecting ten. Primary theme is mobility (double jump and slide). Secondary theme is timing. I designed it as a skill-theme map: every challenge should ask for at least one of those verbs, often stacked, with intensity climbing as the same verbs get stricter.

Design Rationale

In Maker, slide and double jump normally cannot coexist because of character restrictions. Equipping a unique attack breaks that lock. That is the level's "no idea is too crazy" hook: air slides, and one-block midair gaps that need a double jump plus a perfectly timed air slide. The gimmick is not a new mechanic from outside the game — it is an illegal-feeling combo the editor already allows.

I do not want complexity (lots of weapons, lots of enemy types). I want difficulty: the same two mobility skills, asked more precisely. Early rooms teach jump, then double jump, then slide in isolation. Mid rooms add damage-aware double jumps (B') and air slides (C'). Late rooms split the double jump around another action (B''), add breaking platforms on a timer (E), and peak at intensity 10 with double jump plus air slide.

Downbeats are deliberate. After a 5, I drop to a 1 (zone 6 is just B) so the player can reset hands before the next stack. The last screen is the exam, not a new toy.

Level systems / cadence

I notate rooms the way I notate Mega Man cadence:

  • A jump / A' spring jump
  • B double jump / B' place both jumps to avoid damage / B'' jump, do something else, jump
  • C slide / C' air slide
  • D timed jump
  • E breaking platform with a limited window

Fourteen labelled challenges across five screens. Intensity is scored per zone, not guessed after play. Examples: screen 1 walks A, B, C, then B' (3), then A+C' (5). Screen 3 zone 10 is B+C'+D+E at 8 — mobility, air slide, timing, and a platform that will not wait. Screen 4 zone 13 offers a fork (B+E or C+E) so the exam still has a little player choice. Screen 5 zone 14 is B+C' at 10, the cleanest statement of the theme.

That table is the technical document for the level. If a room cannot be named in cadence, it does not belong on the map.
